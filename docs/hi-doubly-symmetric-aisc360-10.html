Engineering warnings

!
Excel parity only — not approved for designThe source workbook states AISC 360-05. This preview preserves legacy behavior for parity (signed Cb, weak-axis Fe and stiffeners). Do not use this output for construction design or claim AISC 360-10 compliance.

Calculation notes

  • Built-up, doubly symmetric I/H with homogeneous steel and rectangular plates. Hybrid steel and cover plates are not included. Hot-rolled development is pending.
  • Gross-section member properties are used. Web openings will be developed in a later phase.
  • External Direct Analysis to AISC 360-10 C2 is assumed. Under C3, K = 1; SAP2000 must be configured for the required method. The component does not verify the structural model.
  • Lx, Ly, Lz and Lb remain independent physical restraint lengths. Nominal E/G are used in member resistance, without repeating the analysis stiffness reductions.
  • For ASD, the external Direct Analysis follows C2.1(4): 1.6 times ASD analysis loads, followed by division of required strengths by 1.6. This calculator does not convert forces.
  • Cb = 1 until the SAP2000 API can supply geometry, forces and a verified moment diagram. Other standards may use a corresponding factor.
