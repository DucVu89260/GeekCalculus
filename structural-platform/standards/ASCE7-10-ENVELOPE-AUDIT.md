# ASCE 7-10 Chapter 28 envelope workspace

Scope: user PICTURE 1–5 UI, illustrated procedure/building catalog and a live
Chapter 28 Part 1 reference pressure report. Existing Chapter 27 is retained as
a separate catalog entry. Chapter 29 signs are not filed under Chapter 28;
directional MWFRS is Chapter 27, not the Chapter 28 label in the supplied list.
MWFRS envelope results are not Chapter 30 C&C results.

Source: the previously registered 658-page ASCE 7-10 PDF, SHA-256
`ae2997f7a31975965b33065ba54acbf4da441f4f821b2a97372d67c2bd1ca248`.
Physical PDF pp.344–348 / printed pp.297–301. Figure 28.4-1 coefficients and
notes were read visually on physical p.348; the screenshots are layout
references, not an independent formula authority.

`asce7-10-envelope.mjs` owns derived geometry, Table 28.3-1 exposure adjustment,
Eq.28.3-1 SI qh, Table 26.11-1 GCpi, Fig.28.4-1 Case A/B coefficients and linear
interpolation, Eq.28.4-1 signed pressure, edge width a (note 9), negative roof
zone 2/2E extent (note 8) and designated T-zone net-pressure reduction (note 5).
Chapter 26 exposure constants/velocity primitives are reused from the existing
wind core. No duplicated formulas are inserted into Vue templates as engines.

Units: geometry m, display wind km/h or m/s with explicit conversion, pressure
kN/m². qh uses 0.000613 with V in m/s. GCpf includes gust response, so no extra
G is applied. GCpi is applied at a consistent sign across the simultaneous
building case. No legacy wind importance factor or ASD load-combination
reduction is silently inserted. Linear interpolation through the sign change
in Case A zone 2 follows this figure's note 2; Chapter 27's separate
same-sign interpolation condition is not imported here.

Eligibility: §26.2 defines SI low-rise h≤18 m (printed alongside 60 ft), and h≤min(L,B), gable θ≤45°. h=he for θ≤10°;
otherwise h=(hr+he)/2. B is normal to the ridge, L parallel. Exposure B uses a
30 ft (9.144 m) minimum evaluation height; C/D use 15 ft (4.572 m). The edge
width SI lower bound is 0.9 m as printed in note 9. Kd=.85 is tied to Chapter 2
load combinations. Input Kzt must be ≥1, without a shielding reduction.
Monoslope geometry is selectable and illustrated, but its pressure branches
remain blocked pending a separate Chapter 28 applicability verification.

The UI's initial input is a review example, with project conditions not yet
confirmed. Hurricane-region selection adds an opening-protection review note;
it never guesses a different enclosure or GCpi. Partially enclosed uses
±.55 without assuming the optional large-volume reduction. Invalid input clears
the tables. Geometry out of scope retains parameters for inspection but
withholds pressure/torsion rows. No whole-building PASS is generated.

The screenshot's qh≈1.114 is not forced into the implementation; calculations
retain the original code SI coefficient and unrounded intermediate values.
The printed 0–5° coefficient row is unchanged; Case B uses its fixed 0–90° row.
Report torsion rows are 25% of **full net pressure**, not 25% of GCpf followed
by an unreduced internal pressure term. Notes 5/6/8 and the eight corner loading
patterns still require project-level checks. Schematic SVGs are explanatory;
the source PDF remains the authority for load assignment, exceptions and all
eight patterns. Minimum-load effect checks §28.4.4 (wall .77, vertical roof
projection .38 kN/m²), parapets, overhangs, structural response, and SAP model
assignment are not certified by this report. Overall `REVIEW_REQUIRED` remains.
